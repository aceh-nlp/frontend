A language lives where it is spoken.

Acehnese is carried in kitchens, on fishing boats and over coffee, far from the data that trained today’s models, which learned about Aceh from other people’s choices and inherited their blind spots. We are a small group from different backgrounds, gathered on Pulau Weh by curiosity and care for the language, helping speakers collect their own words and decide what counts as data, who checks it, and what it is for. This is how we begin to decolonise the technology: a language model held by a community becomes a living archive, one you can question, correct and teach, so knowledge keeps moving, as it always has, from one voice to the next.

Why this matters

About 3.4 million people speak Acehnese, yet almost no open, transcribed speech exists for it. Existing models were trained mostly on Bible text and stumble on everyday conversation.

In our own tests, models produced Acehnese that native speakers could recognise but would still correct. That polish has to come from the community itself, so the project starts with people, and the models become a tool the community shapes and uses.

How it works

It grows like a coconut palm, and its fruit starts the next round.

1Seed

The seed holds what is alive.

It starts with sentences specialists have checked: only a few, but they show what good Acehnese sounds like.

2Roots

The roots reach out.

Acehnese text and speech are gathered, sorted by how far they can be trusted.

  • Specialist-checked

    Sentences checked and corrected by educated native speakers from NGOs and institutions. The best data, and the smallest.

  • Everyday speakers

    Daily speakers, recordings and internet text, with a quick check from speakers.

  • Synthetic

    AI-drafted sentences, clearly marked as unverified. The most plentiful and the least trusted — until a speaker corrects them.

Quality goes up the list; volume goes down. Nothing moves up a layer without a human.

3Palm

The palm grows, and is tasted.

Checked data trains a language model. Speakers check new data and model answers in the same way.
